PetitionsManager brings together petitions from 11 independent platforms in one place. You can filter, search, mark petitions as signed and archive them. The app is free, contains no advertising and stores all your data locally on your device only — there is no account and no server.

Petition data is synchronised once a day automatically from the respective platforms and is then available offline.

<b>Features</b>

* Petitions from all enabled platforms in a single list
* Full-text search across platforms, with suggestions as you type
* Find related petitions — via shared tags and overlapping text
* Filter by tags; tags are derived automatically from titles and texts
* Pin platforms as favourites
* Swipe gestures: right to mark as signed, left to archive
* Two layouts: Relief in neumorphic style, Magazine as a tile grid
* Light and dark theme, accent colour of your choice
* Daily reminder at a time you choose, optionally only when there are new petitions
* Bilingual interface (German, English), defaults to your device language
* Export your data and read it back in
* Fully usable offline

<b>Which platforms</b>

350.org, Avaaz, Bundestag ePetitionen, Change.org, Ekō, foodwatch, Innn.it, OpenPetition, WeAct, WeMove Europe and the European Parliament Committee on Petitions.

<b>What the app does not do</b>

PetitionsManager is an independent tool and is not affiliated with any of the listed platforms. Petitions are signed exclusively on the respective platform itself — the app does not transmit any signatures and does not act on your behalf.

<b>Privacy</b>

There is no user account, no sign-in and no server of our own. Favourites, markings and settings stay on your device. For the daily sync the app downloads the published petition data; the preview images of the petitions are hosted on the servers of the respective platform.

<b>Note on language</b>

Most petitions come from German-language platforms, so their titles and texts are in German. The interface is fully available in English.
